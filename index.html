Oat & Banana Cookies

12 minutes • guilt-free

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • Handful of dark chocolate chips (optional)
  • Pinch of cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Mash the bananas well.
  2. Mix in the oats and cinnamon.
  3. Scoop onto a tray and bake at 180°C for 12 minutes.
