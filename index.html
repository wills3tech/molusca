Oat & Banana Cookies

12 minutes • Guilt-free alternative

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe organic bananas
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • Handful of dark cacao nibs
  • Pinch of Ceylon cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Mash the ripe bananas thoroughly in a mixing bowl.
  2. Fold in the rolled oats and Ceylon cinnamon until uniform.
  3. Shape into small cookies on parchment paper.
  4. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 12 minutes until golden.
